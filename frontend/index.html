How to dictate — say all of this in one recording

  1. Specimen type, e.g. "Blood."
  2. Specimen site of collection, e.g. "Left arm vein."
  3. Date of collection, e.g. "5 October 2026."
  4. Time of collection, e.g. "10:30 AM" — always say AM or PM.
  5. Reason for request, e.g. "Suspected anaemia."
  6. Hospital or clinic name, e.g. "Greenside Clinic."
  7. Ward, e.g. "Medical Ward 3B."
  8. Patient hospital number, e.g. "H123456."
  9. Clinical history, e.g. "Fatigue and weakness for four days."
  10. Provisional diagnosis, e.g. "Iron deficiency anaemia."
  11. Tests required, e.g. "Full blood count, U and E."
  12. Priority, e.g. "Routine" or "Urgent."

Say each label, then a comma, then the answer — exactly like the examples above. After you press Stop, the fields you dictated appear below for you to check; anything missing or unclear will be listed so you can fix it without re-recording.
